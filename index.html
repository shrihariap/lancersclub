Frequently asked questions

What does Lancers Club do?

We build custom software and websites, integrate business systems, process and report on data, and provide business and technology consulting. Most engagements combine two or more of these, for example a web application with integrations to an existing CRM and a reporting layer on top.

Can you work with systems and code we already have?

Yes. Much of our work starts from an existing system: integrating it with other tools, adding reporting on top of it, or modernising parts of it without a full rewrite. We begin with a short review of what you have before recommending changes.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with businesses in India and abroad. Working hours, communication cadence and tooling are agreed at the start of each engagement so collaboration across time zones runs smoothly.

How do you price work?

Fixed-scope projects are quoted after a short discovery phase, with scope and price agreed in writing before work begins. Dedicated teams and consulting retainers are billed monthly. There are no hidden fees, and changes to scope are discussed and agreed before they affect cost.

Which technologies do you use?

We choose based on your requirements and your team’s ability to maintain the result. Common choices include React and Next.js, Node.js and Python on the backend, PostgreSQL, and AWS or Google Cloud for hosting, with platforms such as Webflow for marketing sites.
